About Me

I think in systems.
I design for people.

I'm Shiva Ram — a product designer from Hyderabad with a background in electronics engineering and a career built on understanding why people do the things they do.

I got into design because I kept noticing the gap between how products worked and how people actually used them. That gap bothered me enough to do something about it.

Where I Came From

Engineering school taught me to think.
Design taught me to listen.

I graduated from Methodist College in 2025 with a degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering. Four years of circuit diagrams and signal theory — and the whole time, I kept gravitating toward the part of every project where humans and systems meet.

That's when I found UX. The Google UX Design Certificate gave me a framework. My own obsessive side-project streak — building apps, design systems, and client work — gave me the actual skills.

I placed 3rd at the Humanity Founders AI Counsellor Hackathon in 2025. Not because I had the most technical solution, but because I spent the most time talking to the people who would actually use it.

What I Do Now

End-to-end design.
From blank canvas to dev handoff.

Right now I work as a freelance product designer — which means I own the whole process. Research, strategy, wireframes, high-fidelity UI, design systems, and everything in between.

I've shipped work for ed-tech, fintech, medical, and brand clients. I like problems that are complicated enough to be interesting but important enough to matter.
